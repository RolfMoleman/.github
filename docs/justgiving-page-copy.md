# 90 miles in October for Mind

## Page title

90 miles in October for Mind

## Short summary

I’m walking 90 miles in October to support Mind, because mental health has
shaped my own life and the lives of people I care about.

## Story

I’m taking on 90 miles in October for Mind because mental health isn’t an
abstract cause to me. It has shaped my own life, the lives of people I love,
and the experiences of friends and colleagues around me.

I’ve undergone treatment for anxiety, depression and PTSD following some very
difficult challenges in my personal life. I’m also awaiting a formal AuDHD
diagnosis, while my children are awaiting their own assessments. Other members
of my family, as well as friends and colleagues, have experienced mental health
challenges too.

That’s why Mind’s work matters to me personally.

Sometimes the hardest part is simply knowing where to turn. Mind provides
information and support, runs confidential helplines, works alongside local
Minds across England and Wales, and provides online peer support. For someone
who is struggling, being able to find reliable information, connect with
others or speak to someone who understands can make a real difference.

Mental health problems are also far from rare. Mind reports that around 1 in 4
people in England experience a mental health problem of some kind each year.
Behind that statistic are real people, families, workplaces and friendships.

So throughout October I’ll be walking 90 miles to raise money for Mind and to
help keep conversations about mental health open.

If you’re able to donate, thank you. If you can’t, sharing the page or simply
checking in on someone who might be struggling still matters.

Every mile is for better support, better understanding and fewer people feeling
that they have to deal with things alone.
