# RolfMoleman brand guide

## Positioning

**Short description**

Platform engineering, infrastructure and automation from down at the bottom of
the mole hole.

**Profile description**

RolfMoleman builds reusable infrastructure patterns, automation workflows and
developer tooling, with a particular focus on Terraform, PowerShell, Microsoft
Azure, Azure DevOps and GitHub.

## Relationship to the organisation

RolfMoleman is the personal engineering identity behind
[Down At The Bottom Of The Mole Hole](https://github.com/DownAtTheBottomOfTheMoleHole).
Both identities use the same underground workshop setting and warm industrial
palette. The personal identity leads with engineering specialisms; the
organisation identity leads with its projects and community.

## Visual language

| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mine black | Near-black background | `#0B0907` |
| Tunnel bronze | Structural detail | `#6F4728` |
| Lantern gold | Headlines and rules | `#D1A45E` |
| Cave parchment | Primary display text | `#E8D7B5` |
| Hologram blue | Small digital accent | `#66D2FF` |

Use dark, detailed workshop imagery with generous negative space. Bronze and
gold should feel warm rather than glossy. Hologram blue is an accent, not a
second dominant palette.

## Typography

Stonehenge is the display face for rendered brand artwork. Use uppercase and
keep the wording short enough to remain legible at profile-banner sizes.

The repository does not contain `stonehen.ttf`. The
[DaFont listing](https://www.dafont.com/stonehenge.font) identifies the file but
does not state a clear redistribution or commercial-use licence. Confirm the
appropriate rights with the typeface owner before expanding its use. Do not
commit the font file unless those rights are documented.

Use the platform's native sans-serif typeface for body copy and documentation.
Do not imitate Stonehenge with a vaguely Celtic substitute.

## Voice

- Practical and technically credible.
- Warm and human without becoming corporate or overly polished.
- British English.
- Direct wording, short headings and restrained claims.
- Avoid generic slogans, inflated expertise claims and excessive badge walls.
